## Brand & Style

The design system serves as an industrial, high-durability digital framework tailored for construction companies, civil engineers, contractors, and specialized trade professionals seeking heavy-duty machinery rental, electric power tools, and concrete diamond drilling services.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Industrial Precision & Reliability:** Conveys unwavering engineering confidence, structural rigor, and technical expertise built over decades in the market.
- **Urgency & Utility:** Focuses on reducing equipment downtime on worksites with immediate availability indicators, fast quote generation, and straightforward technical specifications.
- **Rugged Modernity:** Balances heavy-duty operational grit with a crisp, clear, high-contrast digital interface that is easy to scan on worksites under direct sunlight.

### Design Movement: Industrial Modern & High-Utility Pragmatism
The aesthetic merges **Corporate / Modern** precision with **High-Utility Industrial Functionalism**:
- High-contrast structured containers with defined hairline borders for rugged tactile clarity.
- Crisp white and muted concrete-gray backdrops paired with deep navy structural surfaces and vivid cautionary/industrial amber accents for conversion hotspots.
- High-density data tables and spec sheets designed for fast scannability.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for high industrial visibility, legibility on field devices, and clear hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#002B49` / Deep Structural Navy):** Used for primary navigation bars, heavy headers, footer shells, and authoritative typography. Represents engineering stability.
- **Secondary (`#005088` / Classic Industrial Blue):** Used for interactive navigation items, secondary actions, informational badges, and active state indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / Industrial Safety Amber):** Dedicated high-conversion accent for primary call-to-actions ("Alugar Agora", "Solicitar Orçamento", "WhatsApp Direto"), stock availability tags, and high-priority notices.
- **Neutral (`#1E2329` / Structural Charcoal):** Provides deep optical contrast for primary body copy, titles, and technical data against light surfaces.

### Neutral Foundation & Backgrounds
- Canvas Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with section alternations in Concrete Light (`#F8FAFC`) and Concrete Card Base (`#F1F5F9`).
- Borders & Dividers: Muted structural wireframes in `#E2E8F0` ensuring crisp box modeling without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Montserrat** exclusively across all scales to maintain the established brand identity while delivering clean geometric precision and solid legibility.

- **Display & Headlines:** Bold weights (`700`, `800`) provide an authoritative architectural presence reminiscent of engineering blueprints and industrial signage. Tight negative letter spacing is applied to large sizes to prevent visual sprawl.
- **Body & Specs:** Set at `400` and `500` weights for seamless readability of technical parameters, machine capabilities, power ratings, and rental policies.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-weight uppercase styling (`800`) is utilized for category tags, machine status indicators (e.g., "DISPONÍVEL", "EM MANUTENÇÃO"), and manufacturer brand badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and a **4-column responsive grid** for mobile devices.

### Grid Rhythm & Canvas Bounds
- **Maximum Content Width:** 1280px pinned with centered margin bounds.
- **Columns & Gutters:** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters on desktop/tablet; 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters on mobile.
- **Section Rhythm:** Vertical section spacing utilizes `space-2xl` (64px) for desktop transitions and `space-xl` (40px) on mobile viewports to preserve high scannability.

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Single-column stack for machinery product cards, 2-column grid for partner badges, fixed bottom CTA sheet for instant equipment quotation.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`):** 2 to 3-column product grid, collapsable filter drawers for technical machine categories.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** Full 4-column catalog grid with dedicated persistent left-rail specification filters (voltage, power, rental period).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes industrial solidity over ethereal floating surfaces, using subtle ambient shadows paired with crisp border outlines:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` base background without elevation; used for general section canvases and data tables.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest / Tool Containers):** White surface wrapped in a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border with a subtle ambient drop: `0px 2px 4px rgba(10, 30, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Equipment Focus):** Activated on card hover or dropdown expand. Border darkens slightly to `#CBD5E1` with elevated depth: `0px 8px 16px -2px rgba(10, 30, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / WhatsApp Floating Hotline / Sticky Filter Bar):** Direct foreground layer: `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(10, 30, 59, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft industrial corner radius (Level 1)**:

- **Base Radius (4px / 0.25rem):** Applied to product cards, input fields, badges, specification tiles, and standard buttons. Communicates sturdiness and machined geometric precision.
- **Medium Radius (8px / 0.5rem):** Applied to container sections, modal dialogs, and equipment photo carousels.
- **Pill Radius (Full rounded):** Reserved strictly for quick-filter status tags (e.g., "Pronta Entrega") and the mobile fast-call button.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Rental & Quotes):** Background `industrial-amber` (`#F59E0B`), text `#1E2329` (bold), 4px radius, padded with 12px 24px. Hover shifts to `#D97706` with subtle translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary Action (Technical Specs / Details):** Background transparent, 1.5px solid border `#002B49`, text `#002B49`. Hover shifts to `#002B49` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Hotline / Emergency Support Button:** Background `#25D366` (WhatsApp) or `#005088`, paired with direct phone icon for rapid construction site contact.

### Product & Equipment Cards
- **Structure:** Clean 1px `#E2E8F0` bordered card with a pure white canvas.
- **Media Box:** Light gray container (`#F8FAFC`) with normalized machine imagery (alisadores de concreto, marteletes, geradores, perfuratrizes).
- **Badge Slot:** Absolute-positioned badges on the top left (e.g., "LOCAÇÃO", "SERVIÇO ESPECIALIZADO").
- **Content:** Brand tag (e.g., "DeWalt", "Makita"), machine title (bold, 18px), quick technical spec chips (e.g., "220V", "1500W", "Peso: 12kg"), pricing per period (Diária / Semanal / Mensal), and full-width amber quote button.

### Partner & Manufacturer Badges
- **Grid Container:** Neutral `#FFFFFF` band featuring official brand logos (DeWalt, Bosch, Makita, Black+Decker, Mirka).
- **Styling:** Grayscale by default with 60% opacity; transitions to full brand colors on hover with crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` divider outlines.

### Technical Specification Chips
- Compact pills/rectangles with `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, 4px border radius, and 4px 8px padding, conveying machine specs cleanly without overloading the layout.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E2329`, focus state with 2px ring `#005088` and 0px outline offset.
- Datepicker fields tailored for rental duration selection (Data de Início - Data de Devolução).
